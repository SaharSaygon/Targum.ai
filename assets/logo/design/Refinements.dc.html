Ligature — refinements
same glyph, six treatments — pick weight, corners and where the color sits
1 · Split
As drafted. T in ink, ת in color — source and target read as two.
2 · Bridge
Color on the shared bar only — the bridge between scripts is the translator.
3 · Heavy
Thicker stroke, tighter counters — more iconic at 16px, less airy large.
4 · Rounded
Round caps and joins — softer, friendlier; sits closer to the menubar's tone.
5 · Mono
One ink — the form carries it alone. This is what the menubar and print see.
6 · Reversed
Paper and color on ink — the dark-mode and app-icon face of the same mark.
